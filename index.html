About these concepts

These are proposed design directions, not the live practice website or finished client work. Lifestyle and office imagery is illustrative placeholder photography; physician portraits and practice information come from the existing public website. Copy, imagery, branding, and clinical information remain subject to practice approval.

The demos do not book appointments or collect patient information. Phone buttons, patient portals, forms, and directions may open real external destinations. These review links are unlisted, not password-protected.
